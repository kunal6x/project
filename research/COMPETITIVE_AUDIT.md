# Competitive intelligence — diligence targets

## Benchmark list and what to study

| Company | Area | Specific diligence question |
|---|---|---|
| EliseAI | housing / healthcare | How many downstream property/clinic actions execute versus conversations handled? |
| HappyRobot | enterprise logistics | What dispatch and logistics operations are genuinely end-to-end? |
| Avoca | home services | How are booked jobs, funnel attribution, field service system integration and cost priced? |
| Parloa | enterprise CX | What governance, evaluation, routing and human handoff are productized? |
| Decagon | service AI | Which complex resolution actions can its concierge workflows safely commit? |
| Sierra | CX at scale | What enterprise deployment moats and success measurement models exist? |
| Covera | insurance brokerage | How does AMS and carrier quoting depth affect defensibility? |
| Rillet | finance ERP | How does modern evented ledger and auditability support safe finance agents? |
| n8n | automation orchestration | What must be orchestrated rather than generated, and how to handle retries? |
| CarSignal / Parrot | auto shop OS | Which job objects, vendor integrations, employee workflows are owned? |
| Marble | restaurant operations | Does back-office workflow depth matter more than telephone ordering? |
| Oracle / Salesforce | incumbent action layer | Which vertical workflow gaps remain across systems of record? |

## Battlecard assessment rubric
Rate 0–5 on product completeness, domain depth, integration coverage, compliance, geographic voice coverage, support/deployment speed, customer evidence, attribution, reliability, distribution edge and price transparency. Every score needs a supporting URL and date; don't pretend demo observations are tested facts.
